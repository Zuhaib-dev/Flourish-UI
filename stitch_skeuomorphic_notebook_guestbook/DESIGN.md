## Brand & Style

The brand evokes tactile warmth, nostalgic intimacy, and creative playfulness. It mimics the sensory satisfaction of handling a well-loved stationery journal, filled with mementos, candid notes, washi tape, and colorful ink stamps. The audience ranges from wedding and celebration guests leaving thoughtful signatures to mindful journalers, scrapbooking hobbyists, and event hosts seeking an emotional keepsake rather than a sterile digital register.

The visual style is **Tactile / Skeuomorphic with Playful Craft Overlays**. It combines high-fidelity physical metaphors—textured book cloth, debossed saddle leather, warm aged parchment, and brass hardware—with fluid modern interactions. Skeuomorphism here is intentional and expressive rather than archaic: physical notebook binding cues, subtle paper grain, deckled edges, and whimsical pastel sticker collages exist seamlessly beside a clean, floating search pill and swift interaction drawers.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic bookbinding materials, botanical dye inks, and faded vintage stationery. 

- **Primary (`#8B4513` - Saddle Russet):** Used for tactile leather accents, debossed titles, stitching lines, bookmark ribbons, and grounded active states.
- **Secondary (`#5C7C64` - Sage Press):** An earthy, dried-herb green evoking pressed leaves and vintage book cloth; applied to interactive badges, secondary action toggles, and stamp ink variants.
- **Tertiary (`#D97D64` - Terracotta Rose):** A warm, nostalgic sun-bleached coral used for primary callouts, wax-seal accents, favorite tags, and joyful doodles.
- **Neutral (`#F8F4EC` - Aged Parchment):** The core canvas tone, mimicking fibrous, acid-free heavy cotton paper. It prevents harsh white glare while providing soft contrast for hand-drawn scripts and ink marks.

### Accent Accents & Stamp Inks
- **Washi Tape Translucent Yellow:** `rgba(244, 222, 143, 0.72)`
- **Faded Blueprint Indigo:** `#3E5C76`
- **Antique Brass:** `#C5A059`
- **Charcoal Ink (Text Base):** `#2A2621`
- **Ruled Paper Line:** `rgba(139, 115, 85, 0.12)`

## Typography

The type system blends tactile editorial authority with uninhibited handcraft:

- **Headlines (Epilogue):** Sturdy, expressive geometric shapes that mimic classic vintage book covers, woodblock prints, and debossed titles.
- **Editorial & Narrative Body (Literata):** Chosen for its warm digital ink flow, bookish elegance, and comfortable readability across journal entries.
- **Microcopy, Badges & Labels (Work Sans):** Clean, utilitarian sans-serif used for structural labels, timestamps, metadata, and bottom navigation controls, maintaining clarity against textured backgrounds.
- **Handwritten Signatures (Caveat, Kalam, Patrick Hand, Shadows Into Light):** Loaded dynamically to render user entries, personal sign-offs, and tape annotations. Rotation angles between `-2.5deg` and `+3deg` should be applied pseudo-randomly to give authentic hand-applied presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in physical book spreads and fluid scrapbook canvases:

- **Desktop & Tablet Displays:** Formats as an open double-page spread or expansive album board. Pages conform to a centered max-width (1280px) with a physical spine trough (24px) down the center axis. Elements may organically clip outside column bounds to mimic polaroids and layered stickers.
- **Mobile Handheld:** Reflows into a continuous vertical journal roll resembling a single pocket notebook page. Edge gutters drop to `0.75rem`, allowing ruled background lines and washi tape to bleed delicately toward the display perimeter.
- **Z-Index Layering Order:** Background book cover > Paper spread canvas > Ruled/Grid overlay > Scrapbook items & cards > Floating bottom tool & search pill > Quick-signature overlay modal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use cold drop shadows; instead, it adopts multi-layered physical paper stack physics and craft illumination:

- **Paper Stack Elevation (Spread on Cover):** Constructed with a sharp inner creasing shadow combined with a soft, warm cast shadow: `0 1px 1px rgba(60, 40, 20, 0.08), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 30, 15, 0.15)`.
- **Attached Notes & Polaroids:** Casts an asymmetrical directional shadow simulating slightly lifted corners: `1px 3px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08), -2px 10px 18px -3px rgba(40, 30, 20, 0.14)`.
- **Debossed Texture:** Elements etched into leather or thick boards use an inner highlight and shadow pairing (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.3), 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`).
- **Floating Command Bar:** The bottom search pill floats over entries with an ambient glow and soft drop: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(50, 40, 30, 0.18), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.
- **Washi Tape & Stickers:** Low elevation (`0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)`) paired with semi-transparent multiply blending modes (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`) to reveal paper fiber underneath.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture pairs softened paper sheet corners (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`) with ultra-smooth pill contours for floating controls.

- **Paper Spreads & Bound Sheets:** Feature subtle rounded corners (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) to feel like organic cut sheets rather than digital boxes.
- **Floating Bottom Pill Bar & Signature Action Chips:** Complete circular rounds (`9999px` / Pill-shape) providing modern ergonomic contrast against the nostalgic paper structure.
- **Stickers & Tape:** Asymmetric border-radii (`4px 12px 3px 8px`) with semi-torn edge masks (`mask-image` or CSS clip paths) mimicking torn masking tape and hand-cut scrapbook cutouts.

## Components

### 1. Paper Cards & Memory Notes
- **Surface:** `#F8F4EC` overlaid with faint dotted or ruled paper grids (0.5px stroke `#8B7355` at 10% opacity).
- **Embellishments:** Pinned with semi-transparent washi tape strips at the top center or angled corners.
- **Stitching:** Bound edges sport an interrupted dash stroke (`border-left: 2px dashed rgba(139, 69, 19, 0.35)`).

### 2. Floating Pill Search & Action Bar
- **Position:** Docked floating 24px above bottom viewport center.
- **Styling:** Matte off-white or translucent parchment pill with brass accent rings (`border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`).
- **Actions:** Houses an inline search input with magnifying stamp icon, bookmark filter chip, and an elevated "Sign Guestbook" CTA button coated in Terracotta Rose (`#D97D64`).

### 3. Quick Signature Popup Overlay
- **Container:** Modal sheet styled like an unfolded greeting card or index card sliding in with a gentle spring curve.
- **Controls:** Includes handwriting font picker tabs, colorful ink well pickers (Saddle Russet, Fountain Pen Blue, Terracotta, Sage, Plum), and an interactive digital doodle/sticker tray.
- **Action Buttons:** "Press Wax Seal" confirmation button featuring a tactile circular stamp look that depresses on `:active`.

### 4. Buttons & Chips
- **Primary Button:** Warm Terracotta Rose or Saddle Brown with slight convex gradient, pill-shaped, crisp cream typography, and a subtle debossed click state (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Category Chips:** Resemble mini paper tags or manila tabs tied with string graphics, transitioning to a filled ink stamp state when active.

### 5. Form Fields & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Underlined notebook-style lines rather than enclosed software boxes. Focus state transitions line color to Sage Green (`#5C7C64`) with an ink-feather animation.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-drawn checkbox motifs; checked states render as dynamic pen checks (✓) or circular scribble fills.

### 6. Brass Bookmarks & Ribbons
- **Visual Bookmark:** Metallic brushed brass clip docked along top canvas edges (`#C5A059`) showing soft gradient reflections, functioning as section anchors or favorited entry bookmarks.
