# Flourish UI Component Catalog

Flourish UI categorizes components into 6 distinct design aesthetics:

## Categories
1. **Shaders & WebGL**: Hardware-accelerated GLSL shader canvases, liquid fluid mechanics, and generative art.
2. **Skeuomorphic & 3D**: Tactile 3D cards, foil holographic sheen, skeuomorphic audio decks, and physical toggles.
3. **Canvas & Generative**: Particle physics, generative trees, sound waves, and interactive canvas drawings.
4. **Motion & Interactions**: Spring-loaded micro-interactions, swipeable physics stacks, and gestural menus.
5. **Cards & Badges**: Distinctive card layouts, identity badges, glassmorphic certificates, and boarding passes.
6. **Inputs & Controls**: Custom sliders, sensory knobs, radial dials, and biometric signatures.
