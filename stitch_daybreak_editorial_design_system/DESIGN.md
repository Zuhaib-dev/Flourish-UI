## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a neo-classical literary creative sanctuary: serene, deeply intellectual, and contemplative, yet engineered with modern interface discipline. Designed for novelists, essayists, dramatists, and narrative designers, it transforms digital composition into an tactile, poetic experience reminiscent of fine bookbindery and quiet morning archives.

The aesthetic philosophy is defined as an **Editorial Overlay Archetype**. The lower plane is a living sheet of tactile parchment featuring meticulously set broadsheet columns, rhythmic margins, elegant drop caps, and delicate typographic flourishes. Hovering directly above this reading surface, interactive controls manifest as ethereal, frosted floating panes—translucent parchment cards, docked floating palettes, and focused manuscript overlays that yield effortlessly to the act of writing.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical book arts, library morning light, and archival deckle-edge paper.

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** The base substrate is Warm Parchment (`#F9F8F5`), shifting down to Cream Linen (`#F4F2EC`) for deep work surfaces and secondary column rails.
- **Ink & Typography:** Primary text is rendered in Soft Charcoal Ink (`#24272A`), delivering legible contrast without the harsh, digital bite of pure black. Secondary captions, marginalia, and muted metadata reside in Slate Ash (`#4A4E54` and `#70757D`).
- **Accents & Tints:**
  - *Antique Terracotta Whisper* (`#9E7D73`, tint `#EFE9E4`): Marks focus states, active paragraph markers, literary revisions, and editorial chapter markers.
  - *Dawn Mist Blue* (`#7D93A3`, tint `#EBF1F5`): Used for contextual assistance, generative companion cues, suggestions, and research drawers.
  - *Sage Whisper* (`#6C8274`, tint `#E9EFEA`): Reserved for version status badges, word count target completions, and structural outlines.
- **Dividers & Structural Borders:** Delicate borders are formed with Warm Rule (`#E5E2DC`) or translucent parchment overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` on floating planes).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a dichotomy:
- **Literary Content:** Rendered in **Newsreader**, an authoritative, warm variable serif with traditional editorial proportion. Extended body text uses optical size adjustments, rhythmic leading (1.6x to 1.7x), and intentional ligatures. Pull quotes utilize soft italics, while display headers reference master book design.
- **Interface & Toolbars:** Driven by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing neutral, functional clarity for contextual floating action toolbars, character counters, timeline markers, and modal controls. Its geometric clarity prevents collision with the literary prose beneath it.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas is constructed on a hybrid editorial layout:
1. **The Manuscript Sheet (Substrate Layer):** Employs a multi-column broadsheet grid (2 columns on tablet, 3-to-4 asymmetrical columns on desktop with dedicated marginalia channels). Maximum content measure for single prose reading is bounded between 65 and 75 characters per line to preserve optical cadence.
2. **The Overlay Layer:** Uses dynamic coordinate pinning for floating docks, contextual action bars, and prompt panels. These elements float detached from the page flow, maintaining fixed margins of `2rem` to screen boundaries.

**Breakpoints & Flow:**
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single editorial column with edge margins of `margin: 1.5rem`. Contextual toolbars anchor to the bottom viewport edge as a persistent frosted sheet with `0.75rem` safe gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Two columns (Manuscript body + Marginalia/Notes rail). Margins scale to `margin-md: 3rem`. Toolbars convert to floating rounded capsules.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Full expansive studio view with `margin-lg: 5rem`, accommodating side-by-side comparative revisions, book chapter navigation, and floating contextual panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **diffused ambient illumination** and **frosted tactile glassmorphism** rather than harsh directional drops:

- **Surface Level 0 (Manuscript Paper):** Unbroken, flat warmth (`#F9F8F5`) with no shadows.
- **Surface Level 1 (Recessed Well / Marginalia Track):** Subtle inset tone (`#F4F2EC`) without borders, demarcated purely by soft shifts in atmospheric tone.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Palettes & Action Bars):** Translucent ivory glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), encased in a fine, 1px borderline of `#E5E2DC`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(40, 30, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 30, 20, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Focus Overlays & Dialogs):** Centered floating manuscripts or companion prompts. Encased in `rgba(252, 251, 249, 0.95)` with dual shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 28, 21, 0.03)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances the sharp discipline of printed paper with the soft tactile presence of hand-cut materials:
- **Manuscript Viewports & Text Columns:** Crisp, straight margins (0px) honoring classic book columns and printed sheets.
- **Floating Toolbars & Contextual Badges:** 14px to 16px corner radius, imparting an organic, polished pebble feel to items lifted above the page.
- **Pills & Selectors:** Buttons within floating bars feature pill-form curvature (`9999px`) to distinguish actionable controls from rectangular blocks of prose.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Studio Action:** Deep charcoal base (`#24272A`), warm parchment text (`#F9F8F5`), 14px height padding, subtle pill-shaped perimeter. On hover, smooth transition to `#3D4247` with a low-opacity shadow bloom.
- **Quiet / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px` translucent border (`#E5E2DC`), charcoal ink typography. On hover, fills with Terracotta Whisper (`#EFE9E4`).

### Contextual Floating Action Toolbars (Image, Typographic Aa, Grid)
- Formed as self-contained floating islands (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), anchored over text selections or docked bottom-center.
- Contain iconographic controls sized at 18px alongside `ui-label-sm` descriptors.
- Segmented dividers between tool groups use `1px` height rules colored in `#E5E2DC`.

### Chips & Annotation Badges
- Muted status indicators featuring tinted grounds (Sage `#E9EFEA`, Terracotta `#EFE9E4`, Dawn Mist `#EBF1F5`).
- Type set in `ui-label-sm` uppercase with `0.05em` letter tracking. 8px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding, full pill radius.

### Input Fields & Story Prompt Overlays
- Framed in subtle 1px `#E5E2DC` boundaries, shifting to Dawn Mist Blue (`#7D93A3`) on active focus.
- Placeholder text is set in `Newsreader` italic, evoking unfinished thought, whereas user-entered text adopts the crisp confidence of `Plus Jakarta Sans` for prompt queries or `Newsreader` for prose drafting.

### Cards & Literary Excerpt Containers
- Surface filled with Cream Linen (`#F4F2EC`) or frosted semi-opaque ivory (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`).
- Rimmed with a hairline `1px` border (`#E5E2DC`).
- Includes interior padding of `space-xl` (`2rem`) to give excerpts room to breathe.
